Mathematics for the Physics Olympiad

Jugantor Dey

This is a complete self-study mathematics series written in Bengali. It starts at class-5 arithmetic and ends at vector calculus, covering exactly as much mathematics as the physics olympiad demands and no more. No teacher is required. Read the 22 chapters in order. Every PDF is free.

Each chapter comes in two editions. The student edition has no solutions; the full edition includes worked solutions to every problem. Download the student edition first and attempt the problems yourself. Stay stuck for at least half an hour before you look at a solution. The number beside each problem, from [1] to [5], is its difficulty.

Chapter titles are in English; everything inside the PDFs is in Bengali.
